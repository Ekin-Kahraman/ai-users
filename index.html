This prototype estimates how many working-age people use generative AI in each country, tracks the comparable public series from H1 2025 to Q1 2026, and compares adoption with an infrastructure model based on internet access, electricity access, and GDP per head.

The main usage table comes from Microsoft's Global AI Diffusion Q1 2026 report. Population comes from UN WPP via Our World in Data. Internet, electricity, GDP, region, and income metadata come from the World Bank.

estimated_ai_users = ai_share_pct / 100 * working_age_population

access_ceiling_pct = min(internet_user_pct, electricity_access_pct)

access_headroom_users = max(0, access_ceiling_pct - ai_share_q1_2026_pct) / 100 * working_age_population_2026

diffusion_gap_pp = actual_q1_2026_ai_share - infrastructure_modelled_ai_share

Microsoft's AI diffusion values are modelled telemetry estimates, not surveys. The comparable public country series in this build starts at H1 2025, so the page shows H1 2025, H2 2025, and Q1 2026 rather than inventing a 2024 country baseline. World Bank infrastructure indicators use the latest available value up to 2024, so infrastructure can lag the AI period. Potential users is a reachability model, not a prediction that those people will use AI.
